## Small signs

Dr Crystal Aguh of Johns Hopkins Medicine describes early warning signs such as a ponytail that doesn't feel as thick as it used to, or more of your scalp showing than before.

## Ask your stylist

Your stylist sees your scalp up close. Dr Aguh suggests asking a stylist, ideally one who has known you for a while, whether they have noticed any changes.

## Why checking in helps

The same expert advises seeing a dermatologist if you or your stylist suspect hair loss, because the sooner you act, the more hair you can save. Regular check-ins give you a record to compare against.
