## Early signs

Some people notice itching, soreness or more flaking than usual where a style pulls. If the tight style stays in, small spots or bumps can appear near the edges, closest to where the hair is being pulled.

## Pain is a signal

The American Academy of Dermatology advises making sure braids, cornrows and weaves are not too tight, and sums it up as "pain equals damage". A style that hurts is not something to wait out.

## What to do

- Loosen or take out the sections that hurt most.
- Ask your stylist for less tension next time, especially where you felt it.
- If soreness, bumps or itching carry on after the tension is gone, get it checked.
