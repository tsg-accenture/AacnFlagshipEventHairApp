## Relaxers and dyes

The American Academy of Dermatology advises caution with relaxers and says never to apply relaxer to hair that has already been relaxed. Dr Crystal Aguh of Johns Hopkins Medicine recommends having relaxers and dyes applied by a professional, and stopping chemical treatments altogether if you still notice breakage or shedding.

## Heat styling

Dr Aguh advises keeping heat styling, including hair dryers, flat irons and curling irons, to a minimum or avoiding it, because it wears hair out. The AAD suggests a heat-protecting product and the lowest temperature that gives the style you want.
