## A guide, not a rule

Dr Crystal Aguh, a Johns Hopkins dermatologist who specialises in hair loss, suggests taking braids out after three months, and taking out a weave or hair extensions after eight weeks.

## Planning your rhythm

Putting a planned take-down date on your Hair Rhythm makes it easier to stick to. Take-down day is also a good time to look at your hairline and edges while they are visible.
