## Common and not catching

The NHS describes dandruff as a common skin condition that is not harmful and cannot be caught from someone else.

## What the NHS suggests

The NHS suggests an anti-dandruff shampoo, for example one containing ketoconazole, coal tar or salicylic acid, used for up to 4 weeks. You may need to try more than one. A pharmacist can tell you how to use it.

## When to see a GP

The NHS says to see a GP if flaking is still there after at least 4 weeks of anti-dandruff shampoo, if it is bad or your scalp is very itchy, if your scalp is red or swollen, or if you have flaky, itchy patches on your face or body.

## Itch under a tight style

Itching, soreness and extra flaking can also be early signs that a style is pulling too hard, according to the British Association of Dermatologists.
