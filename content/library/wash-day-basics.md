## How often to wash

The American Academy of Dermatology suggests washing Black hair once a week or every other week.

## Condition every time

The same guidance says to use conditioner every time you wash, and suggests a hot oil treatment twice a month.

## If you use heat afterwards

Put on a heat-protecting product first, and use the lowest temperature setting that gives you the style you want.
