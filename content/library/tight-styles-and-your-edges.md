## What constant pulling does

Hair that is pulled on for a long time can start to fall out. Dermatologists call this traction alopecia. The British Association of Dermatologists lists tight hairstyles as the most common cause: tight ponytails and buns, weaves and extensions, and tight braids such as cornrows and locs. Chemical relaxers, tight head scarves and very long, heavy hair can add to the strain.

## Why acting early matters

If the pulling is spotted early and the tight styles stop, the hair can grow back completely. Pulling that goes on for a long time can make the hair loss permanent. Stopping the pulling is the most important step.

## What you can do

- Choose looser styles. Johns Hopkins dermatologist Dr Crystal Aguh suggests asking your stylist for looser braids or locs.
- Avoid putting chemical relaxers and a lot of heat on hair that is already under tension.
- Protect any thin or bare patches from the sun with sunscreen or a hat.
