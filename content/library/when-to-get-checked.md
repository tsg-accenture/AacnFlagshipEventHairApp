## Start with your GP

The NHS says to see a GP if you are worried about your hair loss. Only a doctor can tell you what is causing a change in your hair or scalp. A dermatologist at Johns Hopkins Medicine advises acting soon, because the sooner you act, the more hair you can save.

## Hair specialists

The British Hair and Nail Society is a group of dermatologists, specialists and scientists in hair and nails. Its website has a "Find a specialist" page. The Institute of Trichologists keeps a searchable UK register of trichologists.

## Taking your record with you

Your check-ins, styles and any photos you chose to keep can be put into a care pack to take to an appointment, so you don't have to remember everything on the day.
